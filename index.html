Qi Fang(方淇)

I am a Founding Research Engineer at Paper Instruments (formerly The LLM Data Company), where I work on LLM post-training and reinforcement learning.

My recent work focuses on making RL for LLMs stable and predictable at scale: training–inference mismatch, off-policy and asynchronous RL, and scaling RL on large agentic models, including non-verifiable tasks with rubric-based rewards.

Before this, I worked on robot learning and autonomous driving perception. I received my MScAC from the University of Toronto and my Bachelor of Computer Science from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT).
